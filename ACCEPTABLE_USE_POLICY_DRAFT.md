# Acceptable Use Policy — RallyMax

**Last updated:** [DATE]

This policy applies to direct messages, community court submissions, shared analyses/annotations, usernames, and any other content or interaction you create within RallyMax.

## You agree not to:

- Upload or send content that is illegal, harassing, threatening, hateful, sexually explicit, or abusive to another user.
- Impersonate another person or misrepresent your identity or affiliation.
- Submit false, misleading, or malicious community court information (e.g. deliberately wrong locations, offensive names).
- Use direct messaging to spam, solicit, or harass other users.
- Upload video that you do not have the right to upload, including footage of other people who have not consented to being recorded/processed by the App.
- Attempt to access another user's account, data, or messages without authorization.
- Use the App to collect or harvest data about other users beyond what the App's normal features expose to you.
- Circumvent, disable, or interfere with security features or rate limits.
- Use the App in any way that violates applicable law.

## Enforcement

You can **block another user** at any time (from a conversation) — once blocked, they can't message you, and the conversation is hidden from your list. You can **report a message** directly in the app (press and hold it) for our review. Neither of these actions currently notify the other user or affect their ability to use other features (e.g. a blocked user can still message other people, or still see you in Friends/Find Games) — this is message-level moderation only, not a full trust-and-safety system.

We may remove content, suspend, or terminate accounts that violate this policy, at our discretion — reviewed manually via reports, not an automated system.

## Reporting a violation

Use the in-app "report" action on a message, or contact [SUPPORT EMAIL] for anything else (e.g. a court listing, a username, or something not covered by message reporting).
